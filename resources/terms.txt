ONLINE FEATURES

Multiplayer stats: on servers with stats tracking enabled, your stats are
recorded by that server and sent to FactionFiles to be displayed publicly
on the MP Stats page, associated with your player name. Stats are matched
across sessions and name changes. If you link a FactionFiles account, your
stats are associated with that account.

Achievements and map ratings: if you link a FactionFiles account, Alpine
Faction reports achievement progress to FactionFiles while you play.
Achievement progress is shown on FactionFiles, associated with your account.
Map ratings you provide at the end of a multiplayer game are sent and also
are associated with your account, along with the level, server and mod name.

Auto-download: when you join a server running a level you don't have, Alpine
Faction downloads it from FactionFiles into your user_maps\multi folder. If
you use the client bot feature, or enable the waypoints download option, bot
waypoint (.awp) files for those levels are downloaded as well.

af:// links: opening an af:// link in your browser hands it to the Alpine
Faction launcher, which can link your FactionFiles account, download and
install a level or mod into your game folder, or download a game demo into
demos\downloaded and start playback.

Servers may record game demos, which may include in-game chat. Server
operators are responsible for how they choose to use these demos, which may
include distributing them publicly. Server operators may choose to upload
game demos to FactionFiles. Where they do, those game demos may be associated
with your multiplayer stats data and/or made available for public download.

Crash reports are sent only if you confirm at the time of a crash. The
launcher checks for available updates on start.
